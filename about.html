About StoryCast & Access

StoryCast is the storytelling home of Noble Family Church Rwanda — built so that everyone, whatever their ability or device, can receive the stories God is writing in our church.

Why we exist

Our mission

Testimonies change lives when people can receive them. StoryCast gathers the church’s testimonies, teaching and worship in one place and makes each one easy to watch, read or hear.

  • Mission

    To share the stories of Noble Family Church in a way that everyone can access — including people who are deaf, hard of hearing, blind or have low vision.

  • Vision

    Families across Rwanda and beyond strengthened in faith through real stories of prayer, love and God’s mercy.

  • Values

    • Faith-centred storytelling
    • Access for everyone
    • Family and community
    • Honesty and excellence
Who we are

Noble Family Church Rwanda

Noble Family Church is a church family in Kigali led by Apostle Mignonne Kabera, whose ministry also reaches people through Women Foundation Ministries. The church has a heart for families: for parents who pray, for children who grow, and for husbands and wives who love each other well.

We meet six days a week. Wednesday and Sunday services start at 11:00 AM; Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday services start at 5:00 PM.

Accessibility

Accessibility features

StoryCast is built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. This is what that means for you.

  • Captions and transcripts

    Every video and audio story has an English transcript. YouTube videos load with captions on, and our own video has a WebVTT caption track.

  • Full keyboard access

    Every link, button and media control can be reached with Tab and used with Enter or Space. A “Skip to main content” link comes first on every page.

  • Visible focus

    A thick focus ring shows where you are on every page. It changes colour on dark backgrounds so it is always visible.

  • Strong colour contrast

    Our chocolate brown and white colours meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text is 15:1 against white; the lightest text we use is still above 6:1.

  • Screen-reader friendly

    Pages use landmarks, a logical heading order, image descriptions and ARIA labels where HTML alone is not enough.

  • Control over motion

    The home page video can be paused, starts muted, and stays paused if your device asks for reduced motion.

  • Works on any screen

    Layouts adapt from small phones to large screens, and the site still works when you zoom text to 200%.

  • Plain-language summaries

    Most messages are preached in Kinyarwanda. Our English summaries help visitors and people who prefer reading.

Get involved

How to engage with our stories

  1. Watch or listen

    Pick a story on the home page. Use the player controls, or press Tab to reach them with the keyboard.

  2. Read and reflect

    Read the transcript under each player, look up the scripture, and think about the key takeaways.

  3. Join and share

    Come to a service, subscribe on YouTube (opens in a new tab), follow on Instagram (opens in a new tab) and share a story with a friend.
