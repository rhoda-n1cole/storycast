About this story

This message comes from day 11 of Noble Family Church’s 2026 teaching series “Umugore mu ihema, umugabo mu marembo” — the woman in the tent, the man at the gates.

Starting from Genesis 18:9, where Abraham knows exactly where Sarah is, Apostle Mignonne Kabera teaches how a husband and a wife are meant to love each other: each one in their place, each one guarding the other.

And they said unto him, Where is Sarah thy wife? And he said, Behold, in the tent.

— Genesis 18:9 (KJV)

Key takeaways

  • Love knows where the other person is — it pays attention.
  • A husband stands at the gates: he protects and provides for his home.
  • A wife builds the tent: she makes the home a place of life and peace.
  • Husband and wife are partners; love and respect go both ways.

Worship and welcome

The service opens in worship before Apostle Mignonne Kabera welcomes everyone to day 11 of the series.

Where is Sarah?

She reads Genesis 18:9. The visitors ask Abraham where his wife is, and he knows the answer: in the tent. She teaches that a loving husband knows and cares about his wife’s world.

The woman in the tent

She explains the picture of the tent: a woman shapes the atmosphere of the home with wisdom, prayer and care.

The man at the gates

The gate is the place of protection and leadership. A man who loves his family stands there to guard them, not to rule over them.

Loving each other

She closes by calling husbands and wives to love each other on purpose — to honour, forgive and pray for one another — and prays for the families present.
