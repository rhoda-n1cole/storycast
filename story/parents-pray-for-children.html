About this story

In this conversation at church, Apostle Mignonne Kabera — Miss Naomie’s mother — and Miss Naomie talk openly, and often with laughter, about family and faith.

Mama Naomie’s message is for parents: your first duty to your children is to pray for them. Miss Naomie adds the children’s side: when children receive and honour their parents’ prayers, they walk in more blessing.

And all thy children shall be taught of the LORD; and great shall be the peace of thy children.

— Isaiah 54:13 (KJV)

Key takeaways

  • Parents are called to carry their children in prayer every day.
  • A parent’s prayer covers a child in places the parent cannot reach.
  • Children who honour their parents open the door to blessing.
  • A family that prays together grows together.

Transcript

Download .txt

The original is in Kinyarwanda. This is an English summary of the main points, not a word-for-word transcript.

Opening

The program opens with greetings and laughter as mother and daughter sit together. The host introduces Apostle Mignonne Kabera and Miss Naomie.

Why parents must pray

Apostle Mignonne explains that prayer is the greatest gift a parent can give. Parents cannot follow their children everywhere, but prayer can.

A daughter’s view

Miss Naomie shares how growing up with a praying mother shaped her. She speaks of the peace she felt knowing someone was praying for her.

Blessing for children

Both speakers teach that children who respect and receive their parents’ prayers walk into greater blessing, and that this blessing passes on to the next generation.

Closing encouragement

They encourage parents in the room to begin — or continue — praying for their children by name, and encourage young people to honour their parents.
