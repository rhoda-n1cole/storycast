About this story

In this time of amashimwe — thanksgiving — Aime Frank and the worship team sing “Nabambanwe na Kristo”: I am crucified with Christ.

The song is built on Galatians 2:20. The old life is gone; the life we now live, we live by faith in the Son of God, because Christ lives in us.

I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me: and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me.

— Galatians 2:20 (KJV)

Key takeaways

  • The old life was nailed to the cross with Christ.
  • The life we live now is Christ living in us.
  • Thanksgiving is the right response to a new life.

Transcript

The original is in Kinyarwanda. This is an English summary of the main points, not a word-for-word transcript.

Introduction

[Music begins.] The worship team takes the stage behind the pulpit. Aime Frank leads the song.

Verse

The song tells of a life changed by Jesus: the singer gives thanks that the old life has passed away.

Chorus — Nabambanwe na Kristo

The chorus repeats the heart of the song: “Nabambanwe na Kristo” — I am crucified with Christ. It is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me. Singers raise their hands in worship.

Thanksgiving

The song turns to amashimwe (thanksgiving), thanking God for His love and for giving Himself for us.

Closing

The chorus is sung again, slower and softer, as the congregation worships. [Music ends.]
