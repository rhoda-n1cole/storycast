About this story

“Thank God” by Minyone is a short song of gratitude, shared on StoryCast as an audio story for anyone who would rather listen than watch.

It echoes 1 Thessalonians 5:18 — in everything give thanks — and suits the thanksgiving (amashimwe) moments in Noble Family Church services.

In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.

— 1 Thessalonians 5:18 (KJV)

Key takeaways

  • Gratitude is a choice we can make in every season.
  • Thanksgiving turns our eyes from the problem to the God who helps us.
