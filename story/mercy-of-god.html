The Mercy of God

“Gutunga Imana mu buzima bwawe niwo mugisha usumba iyindi” — having God is the greatest blessing

About this story

Pastor Frank Karemera teaches that the greatest blessing a person can have is not money, position or possessions — it is God Himself.

At the centre of the message is God’s mercy: we are not consumed because His compassion does not fail, and His mercy is new every morning.

It is of the LORD’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

— Lamentations 3:22–23 (KJV)

Key takeaways

  • Having God in your life is the blessing above every other blessing.
  • We stand because of God’s mercy, not because of our own strength.
  • His mercy is new every morning — yesterday’s failure is not the end.

Transcript

Download .txt

The original is in Kinyarwanda. This is an English summary of the main points, not a word-for-word transcript.

Introduction

Pastor Frank Karemera greets the church and introduces his theme: having God in your life is the greatest blessing.

What people call blessing

He talks about the things people often chase as blessings — wealth, success, status — and explains why they cannot satisfy without God.

The mercy of God

He reads from Lamentations 3: we are not consumed because of the Lord’s mercy. God’s compassion does not run out.

New every morning

He encourages listeners who feel they have failed: God’s mercy is new every morning, and He gives a fresh start.

Response

He closes with prayer, inviting everyone to receive God as their greatest treasure.
