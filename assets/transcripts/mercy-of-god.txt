The Mercy of God
“Gutunga Imana mu buzima bwawe niwo mugisha usumba iyindi” — having God is the greatest blessing

Speaker: Pastor Frank Karemera
Length: 1:08:05
Published: 19 Oct 2025
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JGMQjo-qFzA
Scripture: Lamentations 3:22–23 — It is of the LORD’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness. (KJV)

Note: The original is in Kinyarwanda. This is an English summary of the main points, not a word-for-word transcript.

INTRODUCTION
Pastor Frank Karemera greets the church and introduces his theme: having God in your life is the greatest blessing.

WHAT PEOPLE CALL BLESSING
He talks about the things people often chase as blessings — wealth, success, status — and explains why they cannot satisfy without God.

THE MERCY OF GOD
He reads from Lamentations 3: we are not consumed because of the Lord’s mercy. God’s compassion does not run out.

NEW EVERY MORNING
He encourages listeners who feel they have failed: God’s mercy is new every morning, and He gives a fresh start.

RESPONSE
He closes with prayer, inviting everyone to receive God as their greatest treasure.
