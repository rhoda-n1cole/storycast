Girl, Pray and Grow for Impact
Ubuhamya: “Ubuzima bwaranyigishije” — Life has taught me

Speaker: Miss Naomie Nishimwe
Length: 41:19
Published: 2 Sep 2025
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFiBUEAIvqI
Scripture: Luke 18:1 — And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint. (KJV)

Note: The original is in Kinyarwanda. This is an English summary of the main points, not a word-for-word transcript.

WELCOME
Miss Naomie greets the church and thanks God for the chance to share what He has done in her life. Apostle Mignonne Kabera welcomes her to the platform.

LIFE HAS TAUGHT ME
She explains that life has been her teacher. Not every season was easy, and some moments brought tears, but each one taught her to depend on God more.

GIRL, PRAY
She tells the young women that prayer is not only for difficult days. A praying girl knows who she is and where her strength comes from. Prayer kept her steady when other things changed.

GROW FOR IMPACT
She encourages them to grow — in faith, in character and in their gifts — so that their lives bless other people. Impact begins with small, faithful steps.

NEVER GIVE UP ON GOD
She closes with her main message: do not give up on God. Keep praying, keep trusting and keep growing, because God finishes what He starts.
