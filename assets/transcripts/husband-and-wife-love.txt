Scripture: Genesis 18:9 — And they said unto him, Where is Sarah thy wife? And he said, Behold, in the tent. (KJV)

Note: The original is in Kinyarwanda. This is an English summary of the main points, not a word-for-word transcript.

WORSHIP AND WELCOME
The service opens in worship before Apostle Mignonne Kabera welcomes everyone to day 11 of the series.

WHERE IS SARAH?
She reads Genesis 18:9. The visitors ask Abraham where his wife is, and he knows the answer: in the tent. She teaches that a loving husband knows and cares about his wife’s world.

THE WOMAN IN THE TENT
She explains the picture of the tent: a woman shapes the atmosphere of the home with wisdom, prayer and care.

THE MAN AT THE GATES
The gate is the place of protection and leadership. A man who loves his family stands there to guard them, not to rule over them.

LOVING EACH OTHER
She closes by calling husbands and wives to love each other on purpose — to honour, forgive and pray for one another — and prays for the families present.
