Praying Parents, Blessed Children
Miss Naomie and her mother, Apostle Mignonne Kabera, speak together

Speaker: Apostle Mignonne Kabera
Length: 2:12:43
Published: 18 Oct 2023
Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J7c1xc2WMd4
Scripture: Isaiah 54:13 — And all thy children shall be taught of the LORD; and great shall be the peace of thy children. (KJV)

Note: The original is in Kinyarwanda. This is an English summary of the main points, not a word-for-word transcript.

OPENING
The program opens with greetings and laughter as mother and daughter sit together. The host introduces Apostle Mignonne Kabera and Miss Naomie.

WHY PARENTS MUST PRAY
Apostle Mignonne explains that prayer is the greatest gift a parent can give. Parents cannot follow their children everywhere, but prayer can.

A DAUGHTER’S VIEW
Miss Naomie shares how growing up with a praying mother shaped her. She speaks of the peace she felt knowing someone was praying for her.

BLESSING FOR CHILDREN
Both speakers teach that children who respect and receive their parents’ prayers walk into greater blessing, and that this blessing passes on to the next generation.

CLOSING ENCOURAGEMENT
They encourage parents in the room to begin — or continue — praying for their children by name, and encourage young people to honour their parents.
