Service times

Services run from Tuesday to Sunday. Wednesday and Sunday services start in the morning at 11:00 AM; all other services are in the evening at 5:00 PM.

  • Tuesday 5:00 PM Evening service
  • Wednesday 11:00 AM Morning service
  • Thursday 5:00 PM Evening service
  • Friday 5:00 PM Evening service
  • Saturday 5:00 PM Evening service
  • Sunday 11:00 AM Morning service

There is no service on Monday. All times are Central Africa Time (CAT).

Accessibility features

  • Watch with captions

    Videos open with captions switched on where they are available, and our own video has an English caption track.

  • Read the transcript

    Every story has an English transcript summary under the player, and you can download it as a text file.

  • Listen on the go

    Audio stories play in a simple player that works with the keyboard and with screen readers.

Never miss a service

We meet six days a week, Tuesday to Sunday. Watch new services on YouTube or follow the church on Instagram.
